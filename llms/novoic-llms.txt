# Novoic

> Novoic is a London-based clinical AI company building speech biomarkers for the early detection of Alzheimer's disease and cognitive impairment. Its technology analyzes acoustic and linguistic patterns of speech to screen for cognitive change in about ten minutes on any smart device. Products include Storyteller (automated speech-based cognitive testing), Dashboard (an enterprise screening-program platform), and a gated Speech API. Novoic is deployed across 50+ medical institutions and emphasizes HIPAA/GDPR compliance.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public catalog; Novoic does not publish its own /llms.txt.

## Products

- [Novoic Speech API](https://novoic.com): Direct, enterprise-gated access to speech processing, automated speech recognition, quality control, and speech-biomarker models. No public developer portal or OpenAPI.
- [Storyteller](https://novoic.com): Automated, browser-based speech-based cognitive assessment.
- [Dashboard](https://novoic.com): Enterprise platform for running screening programs across sites with AI-assisted triage and analytics.

## Open source

- [surfboard](https://pypi.org/project/surfboard/): Novoic's audio feature-extraction library (Python, `pip install surfboard`).
- [blabla](https://pypi.org/project/blabla/): Novoic's linguistic feature-extraction library (Python, `pip install blabla`).
- [GitHub organization](https://github.com/novoic): surfboard, blabla, and accompanying INTERSPEECH 2020 notebooks (archived).
